## 👋 About Me

I'm **Sahibzada Allahyar** — a theoretical physicist turned AI engineer, building **open-source AI** to make cutting-edge intelligence accessible to everyone, everywhere. I studied theoretical physics at the **University of Cambridge** and now work at **Fastino Labs**.

- 🔭 **Building** open-source AI agent frameworks — **3,000+ GitHub stars across repositories I authored from scratch**
- 🧠 **Researching** frontier LLM systems, agents, and inference at Fastino Labs
- 🌍 **Mission:** democratize AI beyond corporate paywalls — for underserved communities too
- 📫 **Reach me:** [sahibzada.m.a.allahyar@gmail.com](mailto:sahibzada.m.a.allahyar@gmail.com)

I've **authored 3,000+ starred open-source projects from scratch** — these are repositories I built and lead, not contributions to other people's popular repos.
