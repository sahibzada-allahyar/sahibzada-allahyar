## 👋 About Me

I'm **Sahibzada Allahyar** — a theoretical physicist turned AI engineer, building **open-source AI** to make cutting-edge intelligence accessible to everyone, everywhere. I studied theoretical physics at the **University of Cambridge** and now work at **Fastino Labs**, while leading open research through **Singularity Research Labs** — a globally distributed collective of ex-Google, Harvard, MIT and Cambridge researchers.

- 🔭 **Building** the most widely used open-source AI agent frameworks
- 🧠 **Researching** frontier LLM systems, agents, and inference at Fastino Labs
- 🌍 **Mission:** democratize AI beyond corporate paywalls — for underserved communities too
- 📫 **Reach me:** [sahibzada@singularityresearchlabs.com](mailto:sahibzada@singularityresearchlabs.com)
